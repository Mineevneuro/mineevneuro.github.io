Clinical work

St. Petersburg, 2020–2025
  • ~2,000operations: ~1,000 in residency, ~1,000 as operating surgeon
  • ~2,000image-guided nerve blocks, about 15 a week
  • 0 → 3–5procedures a day in the pain centre we set up
  • ~60patients in an mHealth remote-monitoring trial
  • Interventional pain centre at Almazov, from 2022. Set up as a team with a neurologist, a cardiologist and a rheumatologist; I was the main driver on the procedural side, without a formal head-of-unit title. Took part in getting the neurosurgical accreditation for the facility, started RF ablation and minimally invasive procedures, and was the only physician doing ultrasound-guided interventions. Co-wrote the SOPs and referral pathways and handed my part of the service to a successor. It still runs.
  • 3D printing in the department. Set up the pipeline from CT/MRI to a sterile guide in the operating room. 10 documented cases; the cervical and thoracic guides were part of a hospital clinical study.
  • mHealth trial with Medsenger, 2023. Remote monitoring of degenerative spine disease and follow-up after conservative treatment, blocks and surgery: questionnaires, activity tracker data, symptoms, medication intake. I was the responsible investigator; the Almazov ethics committee approved the protocol and the informed consent form in May 2023.
  • Surgery: decompression, fusion, ACDF, spine and peripheral nerve tumours, nerve trauma, cranioplasty with titanium implants. Pain: X-ray and ultrasound-guided injections, thermal and pulsed RF ablation, vertebral augmentation.
  • Nothing blind: pedicle screw fixation was done under C-arm fluoroscopy or navigation, blocks and injections under ultrasound or C-arm.
  • Trained 4 residents. Russian Federation medical licence, neurosurgery, since 2022.

Surgical technology I used

Navigation
Medtronic StealthStation S7 (optical): ~100 navigated operations, 2020–2025 (my estimate). Took part in the department's first frameless navigated brain biopsy, 2020: registration error 1.7 mm.
AR navigation
Microsoft HoloLens 2, Medgital Soft. Software testing at Almazov: trial operations with AR navigation.
Imaging
C-arm (Philips Zenition 70)
Ultrasound
Philips Lumify (handheld: transducer + tablet); GE cardiac ultrasound system. Used for nerve blocks and pain procedures
3D printing
Picaso Designer X Pro (FDM), PLA, Sterrad sterilisation. 10 cases, 2021–2025; ~10 paediatric cranial cases in residency: cranial vault distraction, fronto-orbital reconstruction, cranioplasty
